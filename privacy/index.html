We do not collect location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and we do not sell data or use it to track you across other apps.

Who can see what

  • Posts and comments are visible to other signed-in diners. In "browse" mode your posts show as anonymous.
  • Messages are visible only to you and the other person. If someone reports a post, comment, or conversation, our moderators can review it.
  • Your phone number is never shown to other diners.

Service providers

Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage), Twilio (text-message sign-in codes), and Expo and Apple (push notifications). They process data only to run SocialByte. Data is stored in the United States.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete account permanently deletes your account and the data above. Photos you uploaded are no longer shown anywhere in the app; contact us to have the stored files purged.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. A check-in stops being active shortly after your visit, but its record stays with your account until you delete it. Reports you file are kept, without your identity once your account is deleted, so past moderation decisions stay on record.

Children

SocialByte is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has signed up, contact us and we will delete the account.

Changes

If we change this policy in a significant way we'll tell you in the app and update the date above.
